# Tuner

Tune your guitar or bass by ear against a steady tone.

## How to use

1. Press the tuning-fork button in the header, before «?».
2. Choose the tuning: guitar (standard, drop D, 7-string) or bass (standard, drop D, 5-string).
3. Click a string's circle: its note plays as a steady tone until you click another circle, click elsewhere, or press Esc or Space.
4. Tune the string until it sounds as one with the tone.

## Good to know

- A4 is 440 Hz. The tone has overtones, so it is easy to hear against a string.
- The panel has its own volume. The tone goes straight to the speakers, not through the song's volume.
- Space stops the tone without starting the song. Esc with no tone playing closes the panel.
- The tuning and the volume are kept for all projects.

## Permissions

- **Plays its own sound:** the tone.
