# Beat grid

Lines on the song's beats over all waveforms, so you see where bars start and where a note falls in the beat.

## How to use

Turn the plugin on in the store. The lines appear in every project. Bar starts are strong lines, the other beats are pale.

## Good to know

- The beats are the metronome's. If a song's beats have not been found yet, they are found in the background when the project opens.
- The pale lines show once they are at least 12 px apart, that is when you zoom in (Wheel zoom). Zoomed far out, the bar lines thin out too: every bar, every 2nd, every 4th, then none.
- A song without a steady beat has no grid.

## Permissions

- **Runs an analysis on this computer:** finding the beats.
