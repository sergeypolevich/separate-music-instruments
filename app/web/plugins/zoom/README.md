# Wheel zoom

Look closely at a part of the song: zoom the waveforms in around the mouse pointer and move along them.

## How to use

- **Zoom:** turn the mouse wheel over the waveforms, pinch on a trackpad, or pinch with two fingers on a phone. The point under the pointer stays where it is. The shortest view is 3 seconds.
- **Move the view:** hold the right mouse button and drag, use Shift + wheel, swipe sideways on a trackpad, or move two fingers sideways on a phone.
- **Back to the whole song:** zoom out until the whole song fits.

## Good to know

- While the song plays, the view follows the playhead once it reaches the right edge, keeping it a tenth of the view from the left. Practice keeps it at 60 %, so you see what you have just played.
- Zooming while playing keeps the view following. Moving the view by hand stops following until you press ▶ again.
- A seek out of the view, or ⏮, brings the view to the playhead.
- On the first zoom the waveforms are drawn again in more detail.
- The page does not scroll over the waveforms, and the right button opens no browser menu there. Add `?dev_mode` to the address to get the browser's menu back.

## Permissions

None.
