# Trim

Practise only the part you need: cut off the start or the end of the song at the playhead.

## How to use

1. Put the playhead where the part starts and press **Trim the start**.
2. Put it where the part ends and press **Trim the end**.
3. **Whole song** brings everything back.

The buttons are in the plugins' row under the player. On a phone they are icons only.

## Good to know

- The song files are not changed: only the two times are kept, for this project.
- Playback stays inside the trimmed part: at its end it stops and goes back to its start.
- The waveforms show only the part kept. The metronome keeps counting in the song's own time.
- Trimming clears a loop. At least one second must be left.

## Permissions

- **Changes what is played:** playback is kept between the two times.
