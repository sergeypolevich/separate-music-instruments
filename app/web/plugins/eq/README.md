# Equalizer

A five-band equalizer for every stem: bring the bass forward, take the boom out of a guitar, make a part easier to hear.

## How to use

1. Press **EQ** in a stem's row, before S and M.
2. Drag a point on the curve: left and right change the frequency, up and down the gain (±18 dB). A tip over the point shows the frequency, the gain and the width.
3. Turn the wheel over a point to make a bell wider or narrower.
4. Double-click a point to put it back at 0 dB. **Reset** puts back all of them.
5. The **On/Off** switch in the window's title bypasses the equalizer, so you hear the difference while playing. The bands stay.

The bands are a low shelf at 50 Hz, bells at 200 Hz, 800 Hz and 2.6 kHz, and a high shelf at 8 kHz.

## Good to know

- It changes only what you hear, never the files. The settings are kept for each project.
- The **EQ** button is lit when the stem has bands off 0 dB and the switch is on.
- The equalizer comes after the stem's volume. A stem with no changes plays without it.
- Close the window with ×, Esc or a click outside. Hotkeys do not work while it is open.

## Permissions

- **Changes the sound while playing.**
